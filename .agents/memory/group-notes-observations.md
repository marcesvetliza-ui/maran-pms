---
name: Group notes in room observations
description: Ownership rule for displaying group-level instructions on linked reservations.
---

Show the current group-level note alongside a linked room's own observations, with clear labels. Keep the group note read-only in room editing, rather than copying it into each reservation's editable notes.

**Why:** Copying loses provenance, can overwrite room-specific instructions, and makes later group edits or newly linked rooms show inconsistent notes.

**How to apply:** Resolve the group note from the current group link at read time in room-oriented views and printouts. Continue saving only room-specific edits on the reservation.
