---
name: Support validation evidence
description: Why support-assistant protocol tests and real-model assessments must remain distinct.
---

Do not conclude that a support diagnosis passed review from the audit counter,
or that a green simulated-provider suite establishes useful real-model answers.
Inspect each review outcome and, when available, the corresponding statement
and finding. An internal inability to finish validation is not evidence that the
operator failed to supply facts.

**Why:** Earlier local fixes to reviewer JSON and citations could pass their
targeted checks while the actual lifecycle stopped after a valid rejection,
before attempting correction. The expensive path and the compact successful
simulation established controller behavior, not the unknown content or quality
of the real review.

**How to apply:** Keep a regression that crosses rejection, full correction and
new approval under the same total budget. Assess real-model behavior separately
with a pinned revision, repeated synthetic cases, positive and negative controls,
and human review. If credentials are unavailable, state that real evaluation
was not executed instead of extrapolating its outcome from mocks.

Do not suppress an adverse reviewer finding merely because it resembles a
previous false positive. Repair the representation or reviewer instructions
and rerun both positive and negative controls.

**Why:** Real findings mixed legitimate excessive claims by the author with
invented contradictions and a mistaken interpretation of a text boundary.
Automatically ignoring a familiar complaint could also discard a valid rejection.

**How to apply:** Preserve fail-closed verdict handling and ID ownership.
Use full logical text for review, retain the unmodified trace and assess
answer quality separately from protocol success.

Prefer a brief, source-backed operational answer over an expanding list of
forbidden expressions. Pair actual failed reports with valid counterexamples
when changing review behavior.

**Why:** The user requires useful operator guidance without fixing false
approvals at the cost of false rejections. A prudent consultation step does not
need every internal implementation detail unless it makes a claim about it.

**How to apply:** Review scope and necessary conditions against source evidence;
keep both negative and positive cases. Deliver the patch and offline evidence
for review before requesting another paid model evaluation.

A required negative label needs an unambiguous material claim contradicted by
source, not merely omitted implementation detail. Ambiguous original reports
remain intact as qualitative cases, with neither compulsory approval nor rejection.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires not penalizing correct brevity or changing
a label just to make an evaluation pass.

**How to apply:** Justify adjudication against exact wording and source before
model evaluation; keep qualitative cases out of mandatory accuracy scoring.
