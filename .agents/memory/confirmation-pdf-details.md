---
name: Confirmation PDF details
description: Layout and calculation rules for reservation confirmation PDFs
---

Reservation-specific information belongs in a compact full-width summary before the general terms and conditions. Show the package, active extra charges, accommodation total, extra total, and the combined reservation total there rather than embedding those facts in the legal terms.

**Why:** The confirmation must make the actual reservation understandable at a glance while preserving the existing one-page branded layout and keeping general terms reusable.

**How to apply:** Keep the summary horizontally efficient and dynamically sized. Calculate its totals from the reservation accommodation amount plus the same active charges used by the folio; do not alter folio or payment behavior.
