---
name: Group personal extras funding
description: Conservative ownership rule when group receipts and room extras coexist.
---

When collecting personal extras on a group-linked reservation, do not assign unallocated master-folio funds to individual rooms by proportion or assume a surplus belongs to a particular guest. If the group has unallocated funding beyond its aggregate lodging, pause individual extras collection until the funding is reconciled and attributable.

**Why:** Master receipts can cover another room or shared group charges. A proportional estimate could incorrectly present extras as unpaid and collect them a second time.

**How to apply:** Use authoritative purpose/source allocations when available; otherwise favor an explicit reconciliation requirement over an estimated per-room cap. Keep physical checkout independent of this financial ambiguity.
